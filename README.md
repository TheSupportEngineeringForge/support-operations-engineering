# Support Operations Engineering

This repository demonstrates my approach to designing, analyzing, and improving technical support operations.

The focus is on building structured support workflows, defining incident handling and prioritization processes, establishing escalation procedures, and improving collaboration between Support, Engineering, and other stakeholders.

The projects explore practical operational challenges, including ticket management, service-level expectations, incident response, support scalability, and opportunities for process automation.

**The primary focus is applying engineering principles to support operations, with an emphasis on practical implementation, clear ownership, and measurable outcomes.**
